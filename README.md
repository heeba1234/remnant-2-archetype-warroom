## 🗺️ Overview

Welcome, Wanderer. If the World Stones have taught us anything, it is that survival in the shattered remnants of Ward 13 demands preparation, precision, and a plan. **Remnant 2 Nexus Companion 2026** is a full-spectrum tactical desktop toolkit crafted for players who treat every Root incursion like a chess match against an opponent with too many tentacles.

Unlike the countless disjointed spreadsheets and ephemeral note apps that drift through gaming communities, this companion was engineered as a single, cohesive command center. It unifies archetype planning, weapon tuning, trait sequencing, boss choreography, aim refinement drills, and campaign-level analytics into one elegant interface — so that when you step through the Labyrinth, nothing is left to chance.

This project is the successor idea to the original *remnant-2-hack-2026-archetype-combat-toolkit*, reimagined from the ground up with a fresh architecture, a stronger analytics core, and a design philosophy that treats the player as a strategist rather than a passenger.

> "The Root does not negotiate. Neither should your build sheet."

## 🧭 What Makes This Different

Most companions stop at telling you what weapons exist. The Nexus Companion tells you **how they behave under pressure**, **why certain trait synergies outperform others across difficulty tiers**, and **which sequencing decisions turn a chaotic boss arena into a choreographed dance**.

Three pillars define this project:

1. **Foresight over hindsight** — Campaign analytics are not just retrospective; they project likely outcomes based on your historical performance patterns across runs.
2. **Craft over clutter** — Every panel earns its space. No feature exists unless it demonstrably improves a decision you will actually make.
3. **Resilience by design** — The toolkit is built to remain useful whether you are at the bleeding edge of a new Campaign or re-running a favorite Adventure for the twelfth time.

## ✨ Feature List

### 🛡️ Archetype Build Planner
- Dual-archetype synergy matrix spanning all combinations, with intelligent weighting for melee, ranged, skill-focused, and support playstyles.
- Prime perk priority suggestions that adapt based on your chosen difficulty and world tier.
- Live skill-point simulator with respec cost estimation across every progression checkpoint.
- Trait point allocation visualizer that highlights diminishing returns before you commit.
- Loadout versioning: save, branch, and compare alternate builds side by side.

### 🔫 Weapon & Mod Laboratory
- Damage-per-second forecast engine factoring in mod slots, mutators, elemental infusions, and weak-spot multipliers.
- Range-band heatmaps that reveal optimal engagement distances for every weapon family.
- Mutator compatibility grid with conflict warnings and hidden interaction callouts.
- Ammo economy projections across long-form boss encounters, not just isolated test dummies.

### 🌱 Trait Sequencing Assistant
- Ordered trait unlock roadmaps tuned to your current archetype pair.
- Priority alerts when a trait investment would delay a critical combat milestone.
- Cross-save trait audits for players juggling multiple characters.

### 👹 Boss Choreography Planner
- Attack-pattern timelines for every major confrontation, color-coded by punish window and recovery frame.
- Phase transition checkpoints with suggested repositioning cues.
- Recommended consumable timing overlays synced to encounter structure.
- Difficulty-scaled variants that adjust expected reaction windows accordingly.

### 🎯 Manual Aim Practice Suite
- Scenario drills emulating real encounter pressure: strafing targets, staggered spawns, and decoy noise.
- Sensitivity calibration wizard that builds a personalized input profile from your own tracking data.
- Reaction-time telemetry with trend lines over days and weeks.
- Session replay summaries highlighting consistency versus peak performance.

### 📊 Campaign Analytics
- Run-by-run telemetry capturing death locations, damage source attribution, and time-to-clear deltas.
- Aggregate dashboards summarizing 30-day progression velocity and build effectiveness shifts.
- Comparative overlays between two campaign timelines for returning players.
- Exportable reports suitable for community strategy discussions.

### 🧩 Quality-of-Life Extras
- Theming engine with a high-contrast mode for streamers and low-light environments.
- Keyboard-first navigation with a fully documented shortcut map.
- Local-first data storage so your builds stay yours.
- Backup rotation with point-in-time restore.

## 🗃️ Getting Started (Conceptual Walkthrough)

Rather than burdening you with command sequences, here is how a typical first session unfolds:

1. Launch the companion and complete the short calibration wizard, which asks about your preferred difficulty and playstyle leanings.
2. Import or hand-build your current archetype pair in the Build Planner.
3. Let the Trait Sequencing Assistant propose an ordered roadmap from your current state to your target build.
4. Run a short aim drill to establish a baseline profile.
5. Play a campaign segment as normal, then review the analytics dashboard to see where your instincts and the data align.

Within a single evening, the toolkit stops feeling like software and starts feeling like a trusted strategist sitting quietly beside you.

## 🧠 Design Philosophy

A toolkit like this lives or dies by its trustworthiness. Numbers that drift, silently change, or contradict themselves destroy confidence faster than any bug. That is why every formula in the companion is versioned, documented, and tested against curated reference datasets. When a value updates, a changelog entry explains exactly why.

We also believe **your data belongs to you**. Analytics, builds, and drills are stored locally by default. Nothing is uploaded without an explicit action from you, and exports are always available in human-readable formats so you are never locked in.
